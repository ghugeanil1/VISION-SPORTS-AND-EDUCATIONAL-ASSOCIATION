About Vision Sports & Educational Association

WHAT WE DO

Our Focus Areas

From grassroots cricket to community welfare — our work creates lasting impact across multiple domains.

★ PRIMARY FOCUS 🏏

Cricket Development

At Vision Sports and Educational Association, we firmly believe that exceptional talent must never be constrained by financial circumstance. Our cricket development programme is built on a singular conviction — that every deserving young cricketer, regardless of their economic background, deserves access to professional-grade coaching, structured training, and a clear pathway to the highest levels of the game. We provide fully subsidised and free-of-charge cricket coaching to talented youth from underprivileged communities across Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and the surrounding regions. Under the guidance of experienced and qualified coaches, our players undergo rigorous technical training, tactical development, physical conditioning, and mental strength building — equipping them to compete and excel at the district, state, and national level. Our goal is unambiguous: to identify raw talent, nurture it with discipline and dedication, and produce cricketers who will carry the pride of Maharashtra and India onto the national stage.

Education & Literacy

Supporting neglected children in literacy, establishing schools and colleges, and providing free computer education for unemployed youth from underprivileged communities.

🏥

Health & Medical Aid

Free monthly medical camps in rural villages via mobile medical units. HIV/AIDS rehabilitation programs, preventive care and plans to establish hospitals in rural areas.

👩

Women Empowerment

Vocational training in tailoring, basket making, handicrafts, embroidery, candle making and more — creating economic independence for women across rural Maharashtra.

🧒

Child Welfare

Nutritional support for children with HIV/AIDS and TB. Orphanages, caring homes, day care centers, pre-school centers and nurturing home environments for growth.

🌾

Rural Development

R.O. Water Plants in rural areas for clean drinking water. Awareness about health and basic rights, and full relief support during natural calamities.

🤝

Community Welfare

Physiotherapy centers, rehabilitation facilities, old age homes, widows caring homes, free libraries, and family counseling — building stronger communities.

💡

Poverty Relief

Relief of poverty through promotion of Basic Education, Literature, Science and Fine Arts. Family counseling and youth rehabilitation institutions for dignified living.

INITIATIVES

Our Key Programs

A range of programs designed to create lasting impact in our community.

1

Cricket Coaching Programme

Free professional cricket coaching for underprivileged youth — structured training by experienced coaches targeting district, state and national competitions.

2

Free Monthly Medical Camps

Mobile medical units conducting free health camps every month in rural and backward villages.

3

Women's Vocational Training

Cottage industrial training in tailoring, handicrafts, basket making, food products and embroidery for women's economic independence.

4

Cricket Tournaments

Organizing cricket competitions at local, state and national level — giving every athlete a platform to represent Maharashtra and India with pride.

5

Child Welfare & Support

Nutritional and educational care for children with HIV/AIDS and TB, plus safe homes and loving environments for orphans.

6

Rural Water Initiative

Installing R.O. Water Plants in villages providing clean, safe and healthy drinking water to communities that have been denied access.
